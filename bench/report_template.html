Method and limits

  • What changes: only battery charge/discharge and PV curtailment. Load, PV output, prices and battery specs are the dataset's own.
  • Battery model matches the dataset's true SOC exactly: usable capacity = rated kWh × SOH, one-way efficiency = inverter × √(round-trip), SOC 10–90 %, export capped at each agent's POI limit, ending SOC ≥ starting SOC.
  • Selfish: each agent solves its own full-year LP against RT prices. Peak-min: one joint LP per week (with a 1-day lookahead) minimizing the system peak. Co-optimized: joint weekly LPs maximizing total value with system net load capped at the peak-min result + 1 %.
  • Perfect foresight is an upper bound. Real forecasts will capture less. Report your agents as a share of this ceiling.
  • Prices are wholesale RT LMP, not retail tariffs; there are no demand charges in the data, so "bills" here are wholesale energy value. Prices do not respond to the agents.
  • Grid constraints are ignored in the LP because no line ever exceeds 93 % loading in the dataset.
  • Load flexibility not included: data-center workload shifting and EV smart charging are a next step.
