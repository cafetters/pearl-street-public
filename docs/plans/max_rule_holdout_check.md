**Max rule as built (in-run):**
- Per step, with c_1 .. c_k the binding calls that price it, each call's relief increment is paid at the highest
  settled price among it and the later calls. The metered deviation from the last nomination is paid at the last
  call's price.
- **Relief cap (owner, Nov 4): the max rule never pays for relief the meter didn't show.**
  - The step is today's 1p at the settling call, `p_k (N_metered − Q_k)`, plus a premium for relief priced higher at
    earlier calls.
  - Where an earlier call priced the step above the settling call (P1 > p_k, P1 the highest price), the premium is
    `Σ_i (L_i − L_{i+1}) × max(0, min(rel_i, N_metered − Q_j))`. Each call's relief is paid at its own price spread.
    L_i is the highest price among call i and the later calls, L_{k+1} = p_k, and Q_j is the reference of the last call
    that priced the step above 0.
  - Two calls with p_B < p_A: `(p_A − p_B) × max(0, min(N_A − Q_A, Nm − Q_j))`.
  - At p_B = 0 this is the zero cap, `p_A × max(0, min(N_A − Q_A, Nm − Q_A))`.
- **Build:** the zero cap was merged as `6cf623c`, the relief cap as `69cde6f` (per-call spreads after review) and
  this check's tool as `f1c7ef4`, and the (c) tolerance as `88bd476`. The pre-registration commit, on top of these,
  is the build every run uses.
- A step the agent never nominated counts as nominating its 1p reference.
- D77 cases settle as today.
- The code is `exchange/core.py` `_peak_settle_max`, and the offline reference is
  `bench.item7_resettle.max_first_call_steps(cap="below")`, its default.

**Pass (all five):**
- **(a) Settlement harm:** in half A, settlement-caused harm is 0 in every window. This uses `bench.d56_resettle`'s
  tolerance and gap split, unchanged. Harm means shortfall against acting alone, minus the DA vs. RT gap component,
  beyond `max(1% × |acting alone|, $10)` + $1e-6. Each agent's settlement is its in-run `peak_settle` `net_usd`.
- **(b) Payout:** half A's total operator net payout over the 24 windows (the sum of `peak_settle` `net_usd`) is
  below half B's.
- **(c) Offline match:** in every half-A window, each agent's in-run `peak_settle` `net_usd` equals
  `max_first_call_steps` on that run's own trace (final binding prices, binding nominations, `selfish_reference`
  lines, metered net) within $0.001 per agent. The largest error is reported.
  - **Why $0.001, not $1e-6 (smoke run; approved by the owner, Nov 4):**
    - The trace records each published price rounded to 6 decimals ($/MWh), as `exchange/core.py` publishes it. The
      exchange settles at the unrounded price.
    - The offline recomputation from the trace therefore differs by the rounding alone. On `jan12` that was at most
      $4.3e-6 per agent (29 of 80 agents above $1e-6).
    - $0.001 is about 230 times that, and a misapplied rule on even one step moves pay by cents or more.
- **(d) Roster:** the roster is complete in every run of both halves and in every M0. It is checked against each run
  header's roster and fails if any agent is missing from any check.
- **(e) Horizon:** half A passes the D58 horizon check over the 24 windows. `bench.d58_horizon.horizon_check` is
  unchanged, with its tolerance `max(TOL_REL × |acting alone|, TOL_ABS)` and its aggregation.
  - It is fed half A's cells with `rules["1p"]["net"]` replaced by the max-rule settlement each run actually paid
    (owner, Nov 3).
  - **Disclosed:** as written, that function reads today's 1p re-settled offline. That would judge the wrong rule.
  - **Fail-closed reconciliation:** the net fed to `horizon_check` must equal each run's own `peak_settle` totals within
    $0.01 per agent, or the check stops and reports.

**Reported, not judged:**
- Half B's settlement-caused harm and its horizon result.
- For each half-A window: `agent_steps_by_path`, the count of steps where the relief cap changed pay, and the $ it
  changed.
- Payout per window.
- All harm, gap included.
- Infeasible-target windows and D57 start-of-slice windows, separately.

**Full skip list:**
- **Windows:** none left out. Infeasible-target windows are judged and also reported separately. D57 windows (peak
  before the first gate, detected as in `d58_horizon.bystander`) are judged and flagged.
- **Agents:** none. The roster comes from each run header.
- **Harm:** `bench.d56_resettle`'s tolerance and settlement-caused split, unchanged. `bench.d58_horizon`'s tolerance
  and aggregation, unchanged.
- **Header and summary keys:** none, since this is a confirmation, not a regression.
- **Nothing else** is excused.

## Tool (built and tested before the pre-registration commit)

`bench/max_rule_holdout.py`, with tests in `tests/test_max_rule_holdout.py`:
- **Loader:** builds a half-A cell from a max-rule trace. It reuses `bench.d56_resettle`'s harm components and
  replaces its 1p rebuild with the in-run `peak_settle` net. `d56_resettle.load_cell` refuses max-rule runs.
- **Check (c):** per agent, against `max_first_call_steps` on the run's own trace lines. This is generalised from
  `item7_resettle.window`, which is keyed to the design windows.
- **Checks:** gates (a) to (e), the reconciliation for (e), and the roster checks.
- **Tested on synthetic runs:** a `run_variant` run under the flag, with a forced pass and a forced failure of each
  gate.
- **Drivers (git-ignored, in the holdout worktree, pinned below):**
  - `runs/holdout/cell.py` builds one run's cell after the run. Half A uses the loader, `max_cell`, check (c) and,
    report only, the relief cap's changes. Half B uses the loader unchanged. Both record `peak_settle` totals, payout,
    max price and the D57 flag.
  - `runs/holdout/verdict.py` runs `verdict` on the 24 windows' cells, with the report-only items and the integrity
    checks. It fails closed on any missing or failed cell.
- **Smoke run (approved by the owner, Nov 4):** one design window (`jan12`, 100 MW, m = 0) under the final rule. The
  tool runs on it, checking (c), (d), the loader and the reconciliation. It is report only and is not a holdout
  result.
  - **Result (Nov 4, `../pearl-street-holdout` at `9bcc0d1`):**
    - The run finished in 7.75 min. Peak memory was 416 MB working set and 953 MB committed. The lowest free RAM
      during the run was 1,149 MB of 8,109.
    - Loader: roster complete, rebuild checks within tolerance. Roster complete in every check (d).
    - Reconciliation for (e): $0.00 error.
    - **Check (c):** largest error $4.3e-6, from the price rounding above. It fails $1e-6 and passes $0.001.
    - **Cross-checks:**
      - The run's telemetry is identical to the design run under today's rule, as expected at m = 0.
      - Payout in-run is $7,418.1914 against the offline estimate of $7,418.1913.
      - The relief cap changed 1,232 agent-steps (−$559.70) against the zero cap, the same as offline.
    - Settlement-caused harm: 0 agents. All harm, gap included: 13 agents, $7,773.
    - Settlement paths: 4,320 agent-steps on the max rule, 80 of them with a settling price of 0. None on a D77 path.
  - **Dress rehearsal:** `cell.py` and `verdict.py` ran end to end on `jan12`. Half A was the smoke run; half B was
    the saved `h2h_c_jan12_h100_m5` (today's 1p at $5, payout $11,057.78).
    - The integrity checks passed.
    - Gates (a), (b) and (d) passed; (c) failed only by the rounding.
    - (e) failed on this single window (13 agents, gap included). (e) aggregates over all windows, so one window
      doesn't predict it.
    - Report only, not a holdout result.

## Run order

- **Where:** a dedicated worktree, `../pearl-street-holdout`, with HEAD detached at the pre-registration commit.
  - It has its own venv (the shared venv's pinned package versions, with this worktree installed editable), so its
    runs import its own code.
  - It was created at `9bcc0d1` for the smoke run. After the pre-registration commit, its HEAD is moved to that commit
    (detached). Before the queue starts, two diffs are checked, and the result goes into the results section.
    - `git diff 9bcc0d1 <pre-registration commit> -- agents/ sim/ exchange/` must be empty. That is the code the runs
      execute, so it shows the smoke run and the holdout run the same simulation.
    - `git diff 9bcc0d1 <pre-registration commit> -- bench/` may change only the (c) tolerance, `TOL_C_USD`, in
      `bench/max_rule_holdout.py`.
  - Steps 4 to 6 are built in other worktrees while the holdout runs, so the holdout worktree never changes.
- **Queue:** `runs/holdout/run_queue.py` (git-ignored, in that worktree), one process at a time. Pinned (sha256,
  first 16 hex digits): `run_queue.py` `d549b55b85c9b558`, `peakmem.py` `6e0242f24d05f20c`, `cell.py` `ba4c7668308aec61`, `verdict.py`
  `4816981ec101b11f`. They are copied from the main checkout's `runs/holdout/` and checked against these pins before the
  queue starts.
  - Claude launches it from its own background shell, as the Nov 1 queue was (owner, Nov 4).
- **One simulation at a time on the laptop (owner, Nov 4):**
  - Before every step, the queue waits while any other heavy Python job (a simulation, a check script) or pytest is
    running on the laptop, checking every 60 s.
  - A running step is never stopped: no cutoff, no timeout.
  - No full pytest runs while a simulation is running. Claude waits for the current run to finish first. After each
    run the queue waits 60 s before its next check, so a waiting pytest can start; the check then waits for it.
- **Peak memory per run:** every step runs under `runs/holdout/peakmem.py`. Wall time, peak working set, peak commit
  and the lowest free RAM go into `runs/holdout/queue_status.json`, and for a run also into
  `runs/<run>/peak_memory.json`. Report only.
- **Per window,** in the table order of `holdout_windows.md`: M0, then feasibility (both configs), then the half-A run
  and its cell, then the half-B run and its cell.
- **Before every step:** HEAD is the pre-registration commit and the tree is clean, or the queue stops.
- **After every run and every M0:** the header shows that commit without `-dirty`, or the queue stops.
- **Failures:**
  - A roster failure stops the queue.
  - Any other failure (a referee stop, a crash) is recorded and the queue moves on.
  - Infeasible windows still run.
- **Time: about 8.0 to 13.2 hours**, in one run of the queue. This is from the plan's runtime table: the min and max
  of current-code runs per run type.
  - Half A (arm C at m = 0, 5.7 to 10.2 min each): 24 runs, 2.3 to 4.1 h. These are the 37 runs since Nov 2,
    including the smoke run.
  - Half B (arm C at $5, 12.0 to 19.7 min each): 24 runs, 4.8 to 7.9 h. These are the 23 runs of Oct 31 to Nov 1,
    with the nomination cache; there are no later $5 runs.
  - M0 (0.13 to 0.35 min) and feasibility (0.08 to 0.12 min): 24 each, at most 0.2 h together.
  - Cells (0.03 to 0.25 min): 48, at most 0.2 h.
  - The 60 s gaps after the 48 runs: 0.8 h.
  - Restartable: completed steps are skipped. A failed or interrupted step runs again on restart.
- **After the last step:** `runs/holdout/verdict.py` runs.
- **Then, and only then:** the max rule's horizon result on the 23 design windows at m = 0, computed offline (report
  only, labelled "behavior held fixed"). No change to (e) is proposed from it (owner, Nov 3).

**Integrity:**
- When the verdict is written, in the holdout worktree, `git diff <pre-registration commit> -- agents/ sim/ exchange/
  bench/max_rule_holdout.py bench/d56_resettle.py bench/d58_horizon.py bench/item7_resettle.py configs/holdout/` must
  be empty, and HEAD must still be the pre-registration commit.
- So must the diff of this file's sections "The test" and "Run order".
- Every run header must show the pre-registration commit without `-dirty`.
