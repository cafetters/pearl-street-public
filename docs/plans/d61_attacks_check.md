# D61 attack runs with D64's defenses on (pre-registered)

**Status: pre-registered (owner approved the draft lines Nov 5, 00:35 ET; conditions 1 to 8).** This file and
`configs/d61/` are committed only after the checks below pass on the smoke pair (sep05, not a D61 window), on real
saved traces (CLAUDE.md). At the
pre-registration commit no D61 attack cell or twin has been run on any of the four windows at the build commit.
Results go in a new section after the runs, with the commit's hash.

**What a pass decides:** D61's breadth over windows (M3's pass line, restated in D85). A fail is a finding for the owner;
nothing is re-tuned and re-run under the same name.

## The test

**Question:** with D64's defenses on (`silent_reference`, D78/D79; `declared_reference`, D80 to D83), does each scripted
attack gain nothing, harm no other agent and trip its named defense, in every window, at the $5 margin?

**Windows:** jan12 (2023-01-12 to 13), jan06 (2023-01-06 to 07), feb02 (2023-02-02 to 03), jun29 (2023-06-29 to 30),
in that order. Design windows, none sealed (the 24 holdout windows are untouched).

**Settings, every run** (the configs are `configs/d61/`, generated by `runs/d61/make_configs.py`, git-ignored, sha256
`400ea9a48db606d7`, first 16 hex digits; it reads only the template and reads no data):
- `configs/d58m/h2h_c_sep05_h100_m0.yaml` (the template of the Oct 31 overnight and holdout configs) without comments,
  with only `run_name`, `start`, `end`, `m0_dir`, `peak_response_margin_usd_mwh`, the flags below, and in attack cells
  `attackers` (and in reruns `settlement_rule`) set.
- 100 MW headroom, arm C, `price_mode: true`, `settlement_reference: selfish_1p` (today's 1p),
  `peak_price_cap_usd_mwh: 100`, imbalance on (scarcity = `price_cap_usd_mwh`, 500, when nominations are on).
- `delivery_record: true`, `silent_reference: true`, `declared_reference: true`.
- **`d62_guarantee: false`, written out in every config** (attack cells, twins, reruns, smoke). Before every run the
  queue checks that no config, directly or through `compare_to`, resolves it to true, and stops if one does.
- `forecast_noise_sigma: 0.0`: deterministic (see Seeds).

**The attacker:** `DC_08` in every cell, the participant with the largest battery power (10,619 kW; read once from the
metadata on Nov 4; ties would go by id). Why: its band (5% of power, 531 kW) is far below its full response, so the
band-based defenses are exercised, and its gains and harms are the largest in dollars.

**The attacks** (wrappers in `attacks/`, built and tested in step 6; every message not named is the honest agent's):

| Cell | Wrapper spec | What it does | Named defense (must fire) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `renege_full` | `renege`, `full` | nominates honestly, runs its selfish plan on every bound step | imbalance beyond the band at the scarcity price, or no 1p pay for relief the meter didn't show |
| `renege_day` | `renege`, `day`, day 1 | the same on the window's first local day | the same |
| `renege_zero_b` | `renege`, `zero_b` (owner, Nov 5) | nominates honestly at A; where a later binding call B prices a step at $0 that an earlier binding call priced above $0, answers B's final round with its selfish net and runs it there | today's 1p settles each step at its last binding call's price, so relief withdrawn at a $0 B earns nothing: the attacker's peak settlement is no more than the twin's |
| `silent_deviate` | `silent`, `deviate` | no nominations; runs its honest plan (answering the price) without a nomination at stake | D78: imbalance charged against its 1p reference on bound steps; D65 counts the calls missed |
| `silent` | `silent` | no nominations; runs its selfish plan | D78/D79: the price keeps moving; D65 counts every skipped binding call missed |
| `outage_stop` | `outage`, `stop`, day 1 | after a day-1 binding gate, declares a forced outage over the rest of that call and idles | D80: imbalance on bound steps never excused; D65 counts the outage late |
| `outage_run` | `outage`, `run`, day 1 | the same declaration, keeps running (a false outage) | D81: battery activity during the outage is a breach and voids the day; counted late |
| `misdeclare_power` | `misdeclare`, `power` | declares 50% of registered power for day 2, runs fully | D81: output above declared power, breach, day 2 void |
| `misdeclare_reserve` | `misdeclare`, `reserve` | declares a reserve of 90% of usable energy for day 2, runs fully | D81: SOC below declared reserve, breach, day 2 void |
| `infeasible_declare` | `infeasible_declare` | at the first step, a forced outage over day 1 and, for day 2, power 0 with a reserve above its starting SOC | D82: the reserve is rejected or voided (`infeasible_declaration`) and the run completes |

An attack that never acted in the run (no nomination dropped, no declaration filed, no metered step different from the
twin) is reported **inert**; its defense counts as not firing.

**Zero-price-B opportunity (owner, Nov 5; pre-registered):** defined mechanically from the honest twin's trace: a step
that an earlier binding call priced above $0 (its final price) and a later binding call priced at $0. A window with no
such step: its `renege_zero_b` cells are reported **not exercised** (verdict `NOT_EXERCISED`), not judged. A window
with one where the attack didn't act: the cell fails (inert). If no window is exercised, the variant gets no verdict,
and the results section says so.

**Margins:** $5 for every attack (D75's interim). Reneging (`renege_full`, `renege_day`, `renege_zero_b`) also at
m = 0.

**Runs:** 60 main runs.
- $5: 10 attacks × 4 windows = 40 attack cells, plus 4 twins.
- m = 0: 3 reneging cells × 4 windows = 12, plus 4 twins (the zero-price-B cells share the existing twins).
- Plus per window an M0 ceiling and a feasibility check (seconds each).

**Honest twins:** each twin is its cells' config minus `attackers` (and `run_name`), rerun at the pre-registration
commit; no twin comes from an older trace. The check fails closed (`INTEGRITY_FAIL`) if a twin's header commit differs
from its cell's, either was run from a dirty tree, or the two trace headers differ in anything but keys named
`run_name`, `run_id`, `run_at_utc` and `attackers`, at any level (the header repeats `run_id` under `exchange`, and
`run_name` and `attackers` under `config`; so the configs, too, may differ only in `run_name` and `attackers`).
Summaries are not compared: an attack is supposed to change their values.

## Pass rule (D61 breadth over windows; judged on today's rule at $5 only)

For every one of the 40 $5 attack cells (a `renege_zero_b` cell in a window with no opportunity is not judged; see
above), by `bench/d61_check.py` against its twin:
1. **Gain:** the attacker's value (`agent_value_usd` in the run summary: energy at real-time prices minus wear, credits
   minus charges, imbalance and rebound charges, peak settlement, and stored energy at the slice's mean day-ahead
   price) minus its value in the twin is at most `GAIN_GUARD_USD` = $1e-6 from `bench/tolerances.py` (float guard:
   above it counts as a gain). `agent_value_usd` is the in-run value, computed in float64 by `sim/run_m1.py` and
   written to `summary.json` at full precision (JSON floats round-trip exactly); it is never recomputed from the
   trace's rounded prices.
2. **No harm:** no other agent loses more than a flat $10 against the twin (`HARM_TOL_USD`, `bench/tolerances.py`).
3. **Defense:** the attack's named defense fires (table above).
4. **Roster:** every agent in each run header's roster has a value in its summary, the two rosters are equal, and the
   attacker set comes from the header's `attackers`. A missing agent fails the cell.

**D61 passes** if every judged $5 cell passes. **Reported, not judged:** the 12 m = 0 reneging cells (inside the
band, reneging at m = 0 is the known finding of the strict xfail in `tests/test_d61_renege.py`), and any
`renege_zero_b` cell reported not exercised.

**Reported per cell, zero or not:** attacker gain; harm to other agents as a count and in $ beyond the tolerance; every
defense counter (nominations sent vs the twin, declarations, `infeasible_declaration` rejects, metered steps different
from the twin, imbalance deviation and charge, missed calls, late outages, outage-activity, below-reserve and
above-power steps, infeasible-declaration calls, voided days, whether prices moved); operator payout against the twin;
the attacker's D65 rows; for a declaring attacker (owner, Nov 5), whether its declarations moved any price before
the void took effect, and by how much: the binding calls whose final price went out after its first declaration and
before the end of its last voided local day (the slice's end if none was voided), the steps where the attack's final
price differs from the twin's, and the largest difference in $/MWh (`price_moved_before_void`); for `renege_zero_b`,
the window's opportunity steps.

**Reported per cell, not judged, at zero tolerance:** the total loss to all other agents against the twin: the number
of agents whose value is below their twin value, and the sum of every such negative difference (no tolerance, however
small); and the change in operator payout (attack minus twin).

**Sanctions:** none. Record only (Q11).

## Conditional max-rule reruns (pre-registered now; report only)

If any attacker's gain is above $1e-6 in a cell under today's rule, that cell and its twin (once) are rerun with
`settlement_rule: max_first_call` (configs `..._max.yaml`, already in `configs/d61/`), after the last main run, one at
a time, and checked the same way. They don't change D61's verdict.
- Expected to fire: the 8 m = 0 `renege_full` and `renege_day` cells (the known finding), sharing 4 m = 0 twins:
  12 reruns.
- Worst case: all 52 attack cells and all 8 twins: 60 reruns.

## Full skip list (shown to the owner before the pass rule is approved)

- **Windows:** none left out. An infeasible or D57 window (feasibility step) is judged and flagged.
- **Agents:** none. The roster comes from each run header, the attacker set from the header's `attackers`. Attackers
  are judged on gain, every other agent on harm against its twin.
- **Harm:** a flat $10 per agent against the twin, not section 8's max(1% of |value|, $10): the twin removes forecast
  noise (same data, same forecasts, same honest agents), so the 1% part has nothing left to absorb. Every loss below
  $10 is still reported at zero tolerance (pass rule, "not judged").
- **Twin pairing:** none excused. The two trace headers may differ only in keys named `run_name`, `run_id`,
  `run_at_utc` and `attackers`, at any level; everything else in them, the config included, must match.
- **m = 0 reneging:** reported, not judged.
- **Zero-price-B with no opportunity:** a `renege_zero_b` cell in a window with no opportunity (a step an earlier
  binding call priced above $0 and a later one priced at $0, from the twin's trace) is reported "not exercised", not
  judged. With an opportunity, an attack that didn't act fails. If no window is exercised, the variant gets no verdict.
- **Inert attacks:** not excused; an inert attack fails its cell's defense part and is flagged as not exercised.
- **Nothing else** is excused.
